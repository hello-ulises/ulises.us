Ulises Carrión, a bookwork in many places, featuring selections from the Ulises: Assembly archive, continues at [JOAN](https://www.joanlosangeles.org/) through Sunday, August 9. The exhibition has received notable press, including a feature in the [Los Angeles Review of Books](https://lareviewofbooks.org/artist/a-bookwork-in-many-places/) and inclusion in [Hyperallergic](https://hyperallergic.com/15-art-shows-to-see-in-los-angeles-this-summer/)’s roundup of 15 art shows to see this summer in Los Angeles.
