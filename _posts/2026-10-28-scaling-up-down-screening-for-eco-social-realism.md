November 14, 5pm–-7:30pm, [RSVP](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/scaling-up-down-screening-for-eco-social-realism-tickets-2003579117074?aff=oddtdtcreator&keep_tld=true)

Scaling Up & Down is a screening event that moves between 4 video works exploring the pasts and futures of large-scale regional watersheds and low-growing primitive organisms like moss and lichen. Following the screening there will be a discussion with the artists Laurie Palmer, David Scott Kessler, Lize Mogel, and Brian Holmes, andJeremy Bolen, moderated by Visiting Assistant Professor, Bi-Co Department of Environmental Studies, Dylan Gauthier. 



* Jeremy Bolen & Brian Holmes, If This River Could Move, 2023 (TRT: 17:38)
* David Scott Kessler, Excerpts from A Soft Bed Waits, 2026 (TRT, 20:00)
* Lize Mogel & others, The beginning after the end, 2026 (TRT, 10:10)
* A. Laurie Palmer, Apple Tree, 2026 (TRT: 10 minutes)



This program is part of Eco-Social Realism curated by Daniel Tucker and is supported by Cantor Fitzgerald Gallery at Haverford College’s [The John B. Hurford ’60 Center for the Arts and Humanities](https://www.haverford.edu/hcah).
