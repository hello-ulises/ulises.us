November 20, 6pm–8pm, [RSVP](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/2003773679014?aff=oddtdtcreator)



Ulises will host a multimedia reading, performance, and conversation celebrating grace (ge) gilbert's Holly (YesYes Books 2026), which is a hybrid image and text collection about the murder of the author's paternal grandmother. Hear from four artists, writers, and thinkers who work across genres and modes to explore the stories they need to tell. Come for the art, stay to chat about genreless / integenre writing and art!



Images courtesy of grace (ge) gilbert and Eleanor Sturm.
