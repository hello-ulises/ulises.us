October 29, 6pm–8pm, [RSVP](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/2003667890598?aff=oddtdtcreator)



Join Ulises for a very special edition of the Dear Reader series. This program will focus on Ulises' foundational text “The New Art of Making a Books”(1975) by our namesake Ulises Carrión. In this text, Carrión reconsiders the book as a series of spaces rather than a container for words, a radical position at the time of its writing. Through a collective reading facilitated by publisher and curator Camilo Otero, participants will explore the conditions and the consequences of the book today through Carrion’s proposition.



Camilo Otero is a publisher, researcher, curator, and writer working at the intersection of contemporary art, independent publishing, and cultural history. Originally trained as a journalist, he has spent much of his career thinking about how stories are organized, circulated, and preserved, whether through exhibitions, archives, or books. Drawn to artist-run initiatives and the social life of printed matter, he approaches publishing as a way of connecting people, ideas, and places through shared acts of research, exchange, and curiosity. He co-founded [Calipso Press](https://calipsopress.com/) in 2015, an independent publishing imprint and risograph studio, and is currently Associate Curator at Americas Society in New York City.



This program is part of “Ulises: 10 Years”, an exhibition and programs series spotlighting the bookstore/ project’s work in Philly and beyond since its founding in 2016, and [Collective Futures](https://www.collectivefutures.info/), a six-week, citywide project activating Philadelphia’s network of artist-run spaces, DIY venues, and community-based initiatives.
