## NYABF CLASSROOM PANEL: SMLXL: Publishing by Artists, Collectives, Institutions, and Networks, with Laura Coombs, Yusuf Hassan, Siddhartha Lokanandi, Manuel Raeder, and Rachel Valinsky 

![](/assets/img/screenshot-2026-09-18-at-2.10.30-pm.png)

September 26, 2026, 10:30am–12pm [RSVP](https://printedmatter.artsvp.com/8d3387)\
The Classroom: Third Floor, MoMA PS1, 22-25 Jackson Ave, Queens, NY 11101 

On Saturday, September 26, Ulises will host a conversation at this year’s [NYABF Classroom](https://nyabf2026.printedmatterartbookfairs.org/programs), discussing conditions of independent publishing and cultural production through the lens of scale—from a single artist and the collective, to institutions and networks.

With Yusuf Hassan (BlackMass Publishing), Manuel Raeder (BOM DIA BOA TARDE BOA NOITE), Rachel Valinsky (Wendy’s Subway), Siddhartha Lokanandi (Hopscotch Reading Room), and Laura Coombs (designer of *Cyberfeminism Index* and *A Sexual History of the Internet*). Inspired by the iconic, unruly Rem Koolhaas and Bruce Mau book “S, M, L, XL,” Ulises’s panel interrogates the ways in which scale affects and bleeds across categories in contemporary publishing, often informing the core identities of those involved. 

Funded in partnership with the Goethe Institut-NY.
