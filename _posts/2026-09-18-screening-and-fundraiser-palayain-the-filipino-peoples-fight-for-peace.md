BAYAN USA Northeast & Anakbayan Philly present PALAYAIN! The Filipino People's Fight for Peace to commemorate the 35th anniversary of the people's victory ousting U.S. military bases from the Philippines. PALAYAIN! The Filipino People's Fight for Peace follows a delegation of Filipinos, solidarity activists, former veterans, anti-war advocates, labor unionists, women’s rights defenders, students, and filmmakers investigating the impacts of US militarism across the Philippines. Organizers created this documentary to share their experiences with the broader public, uncovering the truth behind the escalating U.S. militarism and the Filipino people’s fight for genuine sovereignty.



Afterwards, we'll share reflections & discussion and attendees can learn how to support the campaign for "U.S. Out of the Philippines". More info: [bayanusa.org/us-out](http://bayanusa.org/us-out).



$5-$10 suggested donation / no one turned away for lack of funds. Stickers, merch, and copies of political education texts will be available for purchase.
