Sunday, August 2, 4pm - 6pm [RSVP](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/degrowth-poetry-reading-closing-reception-tickets-1994246252224)

Join Studio 105 at Ray Philly and Ulises for the closing reception of (de)growth, [Kaitlin Pomerantz](http://www.kaitlinpomerantz.com/)'s solo exhibition, with an afternoon of poetry. Poets Claire Wilcox, Pearl Kan, and Tamiko Beyer will read from 4pm to 5pm, followed by a reception. 

About the Poets\
Claire Wilcox's writing has appeared in Prelude, Bomb, Makhzin, and No, Dear, among other publications. Originally from New York City, she now lives and works on Oʻahu.

\
Pearl Kan's poems have been published in the New York Review of Books and Posit Journal. Her first book of poems, My Uppalavanna, will be published by Lavender Ink in September 2026. Pearl was ordained as a Zen Buddhist priest by Norman Fischer in June 2026 and practices with the Everyday Zen Foundation. She lives in Santa Cruz, California with her family.

\
[Tamiko Beyer](https://www.tamikobeyer.com/)'s books include Last Days and We Come Elemental, and she is a co-editor of Poetry As Spellcasting: Poems, Essays, And Prompts For Manifesting Liberation And Reclaiming Power. A social justice communications writer and strategist, she lives with her human, feline, and plant family on the edge of the Wissahickon on unceded Lenapehoking, currently called Philadelphia.
